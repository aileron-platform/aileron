# Team Wiki Agent Instructions — Personal Growth

This knowledge base tracks personal goals, habits, and reflections. Be honest and private — this wiki is for the owner, not an audience.

## Rules

- Write durable wiki pages under `wiki/`.
- Store journal entries, articles, and podcast notes under `raw/sources/`.
- Keep `wiki/index.md`, `wiki/overview.md`, and `wiki/log.md` current after indexing.
- Update goal progress fields regularly; stale data is worse than no data.
- Distinguish between outcome goals (what you want) and process goals (what you will do).
- Do not remove source files unless explicitly instructed.
