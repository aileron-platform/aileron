# Team Wiki Agent Instructions — Business / Team

This knowledge base is a team wiki for business operations. Capture meetings, record decisions with context, track projects, and maintain stakeholder context.

## Rules

- Write durable wiki pages under `wiki/`.
- Store meeting transcripts, documents, and reports under `raw/sources/`.
- Keep `wiki/index.md`, `wiki/overview.md`, and `wiki/log.md` current after indexing.
- Action items must have a named owner and due date to be actionable.
- Decision pages capture context and consequences, not just the decision itself.
- Do not remove source files unless explicitly instructed.
