# Team Wiki Agent Instructions — Research Deep-Dive

This knowledge base tracks a research project. Maintain a living thesis, record findings with evidence, and cross-reference sources rigorously.

## Rules

- Write durable wiki pages under `wiki/`.
- Store original source material under `raw/sources/` or `raw/assets/`.
- Keep `wiki/index.md`, `wiki/overview.md`, and `wiki/log.md` current after indexing.
- Use wikilinks for relationships, such as `[[concepts/example]]`.
- Track the working hypothesis in `wiki/thesis/` and update it as evidence accumulates.
- Every finding page must cite its source via `sources:` frontmatter.
- Do not remove source files unless explicitly instructed.
