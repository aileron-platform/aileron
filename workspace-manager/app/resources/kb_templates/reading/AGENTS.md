# Team Wiki Agent Instructions — Reading

This knowledge base tracks a book or series. Build character pages, follow theme threads, and summarise each chapter as reading progresses.

## Rules

- Write durable wiki pages under `wiki/`.
- Store original source material under `raw/sources/` or `raw/assets/`.
- Keep `wiki/index.md`, `wiki/overview.md`, and `wiki/log.md` current after indexing.
- Use wikilinks for relationships, such as `[[characters/elizabeth-bennet]]`.
- Note page numbers for important quotes to enable re-finding later.
- Do not remove source files unless explicitly instructed.
